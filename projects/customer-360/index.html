Section 01

1. Overview & Business Context

Most CRM and customer analytics projects assume clean, pre-joined Kaggle tables. In reality, enterprise customer data is fragmented across app orders, web sessions, loyalty tiers, customer support tickets, and campaign logs. When marketing teams blast upsell promotions without checking real-time support ticket status or attribute drift, they annoy frustrated VIP customers and accelerate churn.

This project builds a complete Customer 360 Intelligence Platform from the ground up: generating realistic multi-channel synthetic logs, running a 58-attribute Data Quality (DQ) validation gate, resolving duplicate customer identities into a single Golden Record, and embedding a deterministic Semi-AI Chatbot for self-service commercial inquiry.

Section 02

2. Data Sources & Star Schema Modeling

The synthetic ecosystem simulates 250,000 subscribers, 1.8 million omnichannel transactions, and 95,000 customer service interactions modeled as an enterprise Star Schema in Google BigQuery:

  • dim_customer_golden_58attr: Master customer profile tracking 58 demographic, behavioral, lifecycle, and RFM+C attributes.
  • fact_omnichannel_orders: Granular transaction line items across Mobile App, Web, and Offline Retail branches.
  • fact_support_interactions: Trouble tickets, complaint severity, SLA resolution duration, and CSAT scores.
  • dq_attribute_audit_log: Daily automated metadata profiling table tracking Null %, Cardinality, and Z-Score distribution drift across all 58 attributes.
Section 03

3. Data Quality & Technical Challenges Solved

  • Identity Fragmentation: Customers changing phone numbers or using different emails across App and Web created duplicate profiles. Solved via deterministic + fuzzy entity resolution rules in SQL.
  • Silent Attribute Drift: Upstream ingestion bugs occasionally populated 35%+ NULL values into key segmentation columns. Solved by building a pre-campaign 58-Attribute DQ Gate that halts segment exports if critical attributes breach SLA thresholds.
  • LLM Number Hallucinations: Pure AI chatbots often invent metrics when asked complex questions. Solved by designing a Semi-AI Router where verified SQL templates compute 100% exact figures first, and AI only synthesizes the executive narrative.
Section 04

4. Analytical Walkthrough & SQL Implementation

  • Step 1 — 58-Attribute Data Quality Profiling: Automated SQL scripts audit daily completeness, uniqueness, and day-over-day distribution shifts before refreshing the Customer 360 mart.
  • Step 2 — RFM+C (Complaint-Adjusted) Scoring: Extended traditional RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) by incorporating an unresolved complaint & checkout latency penalty.
  • Step 3 — Next-Best-Action Matrix: Segmented customers into actionable commercial quadrants (e.g., Healthy VIPs for Upsell vs. Frustrated VIPs for Immediate Concierge Suppression).
  • Step 4 — Semi-AI Query Co-Pilot: Integrated a natural-language interface in Streamlit where CRM managers can ask cohort questions and receive SQL-verified tables + AI action plans.
-- Production SQL Validation & Analytical Query
WITH customer_360 AS (
    SELECT c.customer_id, c.segment_tier, c.dq_trust_score,
           COUNT(DISTINCT o.order_id) AS orders_90d,
           SUM(o.net_revenue_idr) AS net_revenue_90d,
           COUNTIF(t.ticket_status = 'OPEN' AND t.severity = 'HIGH') AS open_critical_tickets
    FROM `synth_crm.dim_customer_golden_58attr` c
    LEFT JOIN `synth_crm.fact_omnichannel_orders` o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
    LEFT JOIN `synth_crm.fact_support_interactions` t ON c.customer_id = t.customer_id
    WHERE c.dq_trust_score >= 0.95
    GROUP BY 1, 2, 3
)
SELECT segment_tier, COUNT(*) AS frustrated_vip_count,
       SUM(net_revenue_90d) AS annualized_revenue_at_risk_idr
FROM customer_360
WHERE segment_tier = 'VIP_PLATINUM' AND open_critical_tickets > 0
GROUP BY 1;
Section 05

5. Key Takeaways & Quantified Business Impact

  • 14% of High-CLV Platinum VIPs were actively experiencing app checkout errors and had open high-severity support tickets—yet were still receiving automated marketing blasts.
  • Implementing an automated suppression rule for open-ticket VIPs and routing them to priority technical resolution reduced churn in that cohort from 18.4% to 6.2%.
  • Eliminated Rp 480M/quarter in wasted promotional subsidies while lifting net retention campaign conversion by +22%.
