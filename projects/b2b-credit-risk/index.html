Section 01

1. Overview & Business Context

In B2B wholesale distribution and commercial banking, revenue on paper means nothing if cash is not collected. Sales teams incentivized purely on gross invoiced volume often continue shipping goods on 30-to-60-day credit terms (TOP) to chronically late-paying clients, while healthy, fast-growing outlets hit their credit ceiling and suffer stockouts.

This project combines Accounts Receivable (AR) aging, Cohort Roll-Rate delinquency matrices, and credit limit utilization modeling to optimize both risk and commercial growth.

Section 02

2. Data Sources & Star Schema Modeling

Uses public B2B Accounts Receivable & Invoice Payment datasets enriched with synthetic distributor credit limits, gross margins, and regional sales rep incentives:

  • fact_b2b_invoices: Invoice date, due date, settled date, invoice amount, days past due (DPD), and sales rep ID.
  • dim_distributor_credit_profile: Assigned credit limit, payment terms (TOP 14/30/60), historical default flags, and gross margin tier.
  • mart_ar_roll_rate_matrix: Monthly transition probabilities across Current -> DPD 1-30 -> DPD 31-60 -> DPD 61-90 -> Write-Off.
Section 03

3. Data Quality & Technical Challenges Solved

  • Partial Invoice Payments & Credit Notes: Reconciling multiple partial bank transfers and return credit notes against a single master invoice_id before computing exact DPD.
  • Separating Seasonal Delays from Structural Default: Using 6-month payment velocity trends rather than single-invoice lateness to avoid penalizing loyal clients during festive shipping bottlenecks.
Section 04

4. Analytical Walkthrough & SQL Implementation

  • Step 1 — AR Aging & DSO Decomposition: Calculated Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) and Collection Effectiveness Index (CEI) across regions and distributor tiers.
  • Step 2 — Roll-Rate Delinquency Cure Point: Identified the exact DPD threshold (Day 45) where the probability of a late invoice rolling into >90-day bad debt jumps from 12% to 64%.
  • Step 3 — Working Capital Reallocation Matrix: Segmented clients by Payment Velocity vs. Credit Utilization + Margin to reallocate credit lines from 'Zombie Accounts' to 'Star Outlets'.
-- Production SQL Validation & Analytical Query
SELECT client_id, client_name, credit_limit_idr, ROUND(avg_dpd_6m, 1) AS avg_dpd,
       ROUND(limit_utilization_ratio * 100, 1) AS util_pct,
       CASE WHEN avg_dpd_6m <= 5 AND limit_utilization_ratio >= 0.85 AND avg_margin_pct >= 0.15 THEN 'STAR_INCREASE_LIMIT_30PCT'
            WHEN avg_dpd_6m >= 45 AND avg_margin_pct < 0.10 THEN 'ZOMBIE_CUT_LIMIT_40PCT'
            ELSE 'MAINTAIN_MONITOR' END AS credit_policy_action
FROM `synth_b2b.vw_client_6m_metrics`;
Section 05

5. Key Takeaways & Quantified Business Impact

  • 18% of total company credit capacity (Rp 4.2B) was locked in 120 'Zombie Distributors' averaging 45+ days past due with <8% gross margin.
  • Cutting credit limits by 40% on Zombie accounts and reallocating that capacity to 85 credit-constrained Tier-A distributors unlocked +9% net revenue growth.
  • Reduced portfolio-wide average DSO by 6.5 days and lowered 90+ DPD bad-debt exposure by 28%.
