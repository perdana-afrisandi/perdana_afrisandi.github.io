Payment Gateway Failure, Smart Routing & Settlement Reconciliation

End-to-end 3-way SQL reconciliation and smart failover routing simulation across checkout logs, payment gateway webhooks, and bank settlement files.

Section 01

1. Overview & Business Context

High-volume digital platforms route payments (QRIS, Virtual Accounts, E-Wallets, Credit Cards) through 3 to 4 Payment Gateways (PGs). Two silent problems drain billions in value: (1) Peak-hour PG latency spikes cause customers to fail checkout and abandon their carts, and (2) Mismatches between internal order tables and T+2 bank settlement statements lead to uncollected funds and Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) overcharges.

This case study demonstrates how Data Quality engineering and commercial analytics work together to audit every rupiah from checkout click to bank settlement, while optimizing real-time gateway routing.

Section 02

2. Data Sources & Star Schema Modeling

Built on a 500,000-row synthetic Fintech Payment Ecosystem dataset modeled in BigQuery:

  • fact_checkout_attempts: Timestamped payment attempts, issuer bank, payment method, PG routed, latency_ms, and error codes.
  • fact_pg_webhooks: Asynchronous callback logs from Payment Gateways A, B, and C (including simulated duplicate webhook retries).
  • fact_bank_settlement_mutations: T+1 and T+2 net settled amounts and deducted MDR fees per transaction.
  • dim_mdr_contract_rates: Official contracted fee percentages/flat rates per payment channel and gateway.
Section 03

3. Data Quality & Technical Challenges Solved

  • Webhook Idempotency & Duplicates: Network retries generated multiple 'SUCCESS' webhook rows for a single order_id. Deduplicated using ROW_NUMBER() OVER(PARTITION BY trx_id ORDER BY callback_ts ASC).
  • Midnight Cutoff Window Mismatches: Transactions occurring between 23:50 and 00:05 WIB often appeared on Day T in internal logs but Day T+1 in bank batch files. Solved via a rolling +/- 48-hour reconciliation window.
  • Partial Refunds & Chargebacks: Separating true missing bank settlements from legitimate post-purchase partial refunds.
Section 04

4. Analytical Walkthrough & SQL Implementation

  • Step 1 — 3-Way Automated SQL Reconciliation: Cross-matched internal 'SUCCESS' orders against PG callbacks and bank settlement mutations to isolate Unsettled T+3 exposures and MDR contract overcharges.
  • Step 2 — Peak-Hour Failure Root-Cause Analysis: Segmented payment authorization rates by Hour of Day, Payment Method, Issuer Bank, and PG Provider.
  • Step 3 — Smart Failover Routing Simulation: Modeled the net GMV saved vs. incremental MDR cost if traffic automatically switches to a backup PG whenever 5-minute timeout rates exceed 5%.
-- Production SQL Validation & Analytical Query
SELECT w.pg_provider, w.payment_method,
       COUNT(w.trx_id) AS verified_success_trx,
       COUNT(s.settlement_id) AS bank_settled_trx,
       SUM(w.gross_amount_idr - COALESCE(s.settled_gross_idr, 0)) AS unsettled_cash_exposure_idr,
       SUM(s.charged_mdr_fee_idr - ROUND(w.gross_amount_idr * r.contract_mdr_pct, 2)) AS mdr_overcharge_variance_idr
FROM `synth_fintech.vw_dedup_webhooks` w
LEFT JOIN `synth_fintech.fact_bank_settlements` s ON w.trx_id = s.trx_id
JOIN `synth_fintech.dim_mdr_contract_rates` r 
  ON w.pg_provider = r.pg_provider AND w.payment_method = r.payment_method
GROUP BY 1, 2;
Section 05

5. Key Takeaways & Quantified Business Impact

  • Isolated a recurring 14.2% timeout spike on PG-Alpha specifically for Bank-X Virtual Accounts during payday evenings (18:00–21:00 WIB).
  • Deploying an automated Smart Routing failover rule to PG-Beta lifted payday authorization success from 85.8% to 97.4%, rescuing Rp 420M/month in GMV at a negligible Rp 1.8M MDR cost difference.
  • Automated T+2 reconciliation flagged Rp 85M in unsettled transactions and MDR tier miscalculations for immediate treasury recovery.
