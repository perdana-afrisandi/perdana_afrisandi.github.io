01 / About Me

Bridging Data Reliability & Commercial Strategy

In real-world enterprises, executive dashboards often fail—not because the visualizations lack polish, but because the underlying tables suffer from silent schema drift, duplicate keys, or unvalidated business logic.

With a strong foundation in Enterprise Data Quality (DQ), SQL Data Reconciliation (BigQuery & Impala), and Workflow Automation, I approach Data Analysis from the ground up: ensuring the data is mathematically trustworthy first, modeling it into clean Star Schemas, isolating commercial root causes, and empowering business users with Deterministic Semi-AI Chatbots that combine exact SQL calculations with AI-generated executive narratives.
